# Optimus Mechanical Archive Film

## Style Prompt
Mechanical archive / premium product teardown: a dark graphite interface with restrained cyan instrument light and decisive orange, blue, red, and yellow component accents. The tone is engineered, collectible, and cinematic rather than toy-like; typography feels like a classified industrial record with generous tracking, thin rules, and measured data labels.

## Colors
- background: #080B0E
- surface: #121D22
- text: #E9EDED
- instrument cyan: #9DD9E6
- signal orange: #E2633E
- component blue: #237BFF
- component red: #FF3B42
- component yellow: #FFC247

## Typography
- heading: Barlow Condensed
- body: Barlow
- cjk fallback: Noto Sans SC, PingFang SC, Microsoft YaHei

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use purple-to-blue neon gradients.
- Do not use playful rounded UI or cartoon motion.
- Do not overcrowd the frame with repeated cards.
- Do not use pure black or pure white as the dominant canvas colors.
- Do not treat the robot as a generic toy product; preserve the archive / engineering tone.
